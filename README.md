Since I started using Zwift, I missed the ability to listen to music that matches my cadenc, so I decided to create such app.\
The app is a music recommendation system for Zwift, which is a popular online cycling and running platform. The app will recommend songs based on the user's cadence and power output during their workout. The recommendations will be personalized to the user's preferences and will take into account the BPM (beats per minute) of the songs, as well as other factors such as energy level and motivational tags.
